An original sun-soaked coastal open-world crime game, built for the
make.fun gta6-01 bounty. No Rockstar assets, names, or maps — Solmar City,
its people, and its story are all our own invention.

## What it is

You are **Mara Voss**, a courier who came home to the coastal city of
Solmar to run parcels and keep her head down. The city has other plans.
Over twelve story jobs you get pulled deeper into Solmar's underworld:
hot deliveries, a bodyguard detail, a smuggling run, a turf war, a
lighthouse job, and a photo shoot that is definitely not just a photo
shoot. Between jobs the whole city is yours — steal any parked car, fish
off the east pier, bet it all at the casino, race the streets, buy a
safehouse, or just drive the beachfront at sunset with the radio on.

Solmar is a real place to get lost in: a downtown of towers and neon, a
marina full of boats, a beach that runs the whole south edge, and a second
island across the bay, reached by a narrow causeway bridge, with its own
beach, palms, and a lighthouse whose beam sweeps the water at night. The
city lives on a day/night clock with golden hour, rain, and storms, and
the same seed always builds the same city, traffic, and pedestrians.

## Systems

- **12 story missions** — First Job, Hot Delivery, The Getaway, Night
  Run, Bodyguard, Taxi King, Smuggler's Run, Getaway Driver,
  Shutterbug, Island Hopper, Lighthouse Keeper, Turf War — with
  cutscenes, timers, fares, and payouts up to $1,800.
- **5-star police dispatch** — one patrol car per star, pursuit
  steering, ram runs at three stars and up, busts on foot, and a
  cool-down that sheds stars when Mara breaks line of sight.
- **Air One helicopter** — a procedural police helicopter that circles
  Mara at three stars and up, rotors spinning, beacon blinking, and a
  searchlight cone that pins her at night.
- **Roadblocks** — at three stars and up the law parks three patrol
  cars across the road ahead of Mara, light bars flashing, re-deployed
  through a live pursuit.
- **Rush-hour density** — traffic and pedestrian bursts pack the
  avenues and sidewalks around the action, with red lights, queues,
  overtaking, horns, and pedestrians who flee fast cars and gunfire.
- **Garage & crew** — buy and customise cars, armour up, and claim
  rival gang turf for $50/minute passive income.
- **Boats, casino, fishing, pets, housing, races** — sail the bay to
  the second island and its lighthouse, play cards and slots, fish the
  pier at golden hour, adopt Biscuit the stray dog, buy a safehouse,
  and run street races.
- **Day/night & weather** — a full clock with golden hour, a night
  city of lamps and lit towers, rain and storms that cut road grip,
  and 15 hidden packages to hunt across the map.

## Systems

- **City** — a procedural grid city with roads, towers, lamps, a marina,
  a casino block, a beach, and a second island with a lighthouse.
- **Traffic** — AI cars that cruise the avenues, stop at red lights,
  queue at intersections, overtake parked cars, and honk when stuck.
- **Police / wanted** — heat earns stars; patrol cars chase, ram at
  three stars, bust you on foot, and cool off if you break line of sight.
- **12 story jobs** — courier runs, a hot delivery, bodyguard work,
  smuggling, a turf war, a lighthouse repair, and more, with cutscenes
  for missions 1, 4, 7, and 10.
- **Combat** — fists and a bat up close; a pistol with a 12-round
  magazine, reloads, and ammo boxes from the gun shop.
- **Weapons** — buy the pistol ($400), the bat ($50), and ammo ($60 for
  24 rounds) at the gun shop on the avenue.
- **Audio / radio** — fully procedural WebAudio: engine hum, sirens,
  footsteps, gunfire, thunder, and two radio stations (SOLMAR FM's
  bassline and NIGHT TIDE's synth pads). No audio files.
- **Save** — progress autosaves to your browser: money, jobs done,
  packages, turf, cars, and every side hustle.
- **Fishing** — cast off the east pier, watch the bobber, reel on the
  bite, and sell the catch at the marina. Minnow $15, Bass $40, and the
  rare Golden Koi $200.
- **Races** — three checkpoint street routes from the chequered flag by
  the garage. Prizes $300 / $500 / $800.
- **Housing** — three buyable safehouses (Beach Hut $1,500, Downtown
  Loft $4,000, Island Villa $8,000). Sleep in one to set your respawn.
- **Casino** — slots ($50 / $100 / $250 bets, $2,500 jackpot) and a
  double-or-nothing higher/lower card game at the casino block.
- **Delivery** — courier side-jobs from three depots, with tips of
  $25–$100 for fast drops.
- **Pets** — Biscuit, the stray dog. Walk up to him, feed him a $10
  snack, and he will follow you — and sniff out hidden packages.
- **Gangs** — three crews hold turf: Vipers, Sharks, and Crows. Stand in
  a rival zone for 20 seconds to claim it; each turf you own pays
  $50 a minute.
- **Customization** — the garage sells six paint jobs ($200), engine
  upgrades ($500 / $1,000), and armour ($400, +50 max health).
- **Story cutscenes** — dialogue scenes with Mara, Dex, and Rosa,
  with a typewriter overlay, at key jobs.
- **Boats** — four moored boats at the marina. Take one out on the bay,
  ride the waves, and step ashore anywhere along the coast.
- **Island** — the second island across the bay: palms, a jetty, and a
  lighthouse you can light up for good.
- **Weather** — clear days, rain, and storms with lightning and thunder.
  Wet roads grip less, so drive like it.
- **Day / night** — a full clock with sunrise, golden hour, and night
  lamps. Some jobs only happen after dark.
- **Collectibles** — 15 hidden packages scattered around the city, $100
  each, with a $1,000 bonus for finding them all. They glow at night.

## How to run it

You need Node.js 22 or newer and a desktop browser (Chrome works best).
No build step, no install, no network requests at runtime — everything
the game loads is a file in `game/`.

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open `http://localhost:8080/` in your browser and press
**Start game**. Your progress saves automatically in the browser.

To play a specific screenshot pose, add a query string, for example
`http://localhost:8080/?shot=drive&seed=1`. The poses are `menu`,
`drive`, `chase`, `mission`, and `night`; see `shots.json` for the four
poses the submission screenshots were taken from.

## How to build the submission

The repository root holds `main.ts`, the entrypoint that hands the game
in. It copies `game/` and `screenshots/` into the output directory and
writes `dossier.json` there with the kit's `buildDossier` function.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

That prints one summary line (game files copied, screenshots included,
dossier size) and exits 0 on success. With no `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set
it writes to `/output` instead. The dossier is built from the game tree
and the screenshots under the scorer's caps (160 KiB of source text, at
most 4 screenshots of 256 KiB each, at most 993 game files).

## The story so far

Mara left Solmar years ago and swore she would never come back. Then her
mother's clinic ran out of money, and the only work that pays fast in
this city is the kind nobody puts on a poster. Dex, her old friend from
the courier days, has a list of clients who pay in cash and ask no
questions — a nightclub owner, a fisherman with a debt, a gang lieutenant
who wants a lighthouse lit, and Rosa, who runs the marina and knows where
every body in the bay is buried. Each job pays, each job costs something,
and by the twelfth Mara has to decide what Solmar is actually worth to
her. The cutscenes at jobs 1, 4, 7, and 10 carry the turning points; the
rest of the story is in the streets, the radio chatter, and the people
you meet between jobs.

## Tips for new arrivals

- **Money early:** the first story jobs pay the best. Spend the first
  $400 on the pistol — the gun shop is on the avenue — and keep $60 in
  reserve for ammo. The bat ($50) is the cheap answer to a fistfight.
- **Heat management:** one star is a nuisance, two stars is a chase,
  three stars is a ram run. Break line of sight for six seconds and the
  cops start to lose you; put distance between you and the nearest
  patrol car and the stars tick down.
- **Packages pay twice:** each hidden package is $100 the moment you
  pick it up, and all fifteen together pay a $1,000 bonus. They glow at
  night, and Biscuit can sniff them out once he trusts you.
- **Turf is a pension:** claim a rival zone and it pays $50 a minute
  while you do anything else. Three zones is a real income.
- **The pier at golden hour:** fishing pays best between 17:00 and
  18:30, when the light is gold and the Koi bite. Sell the bucket at
  the marina before you spend it all at the casino.
- **Wet roads:** after rain or a storm, grip drops. Brake earlier, and
  do not try the tight downtown corners at full speed.
- **Sleep somewhere:** a safehouse is not just a door — sleeping in one
  sets where you wake up after a WASTED screen, instead of the hospital.

```text
                   writes dossier.json)

game/index.html    page shell, import map, HUD DOM
game/src/main.js   boot, game loop, input, camera
game/src/shots.js  the five ?shot= screenshot poses
game/src/          one module per system: city, island, weather,
  daynight, missions, story, delivery, races, hud, player,
  vehicles, save, utils, traffic, police, pedestrians, combat,
  weapons, audio, casino, fishing, boats, gangs, housing,
  collectibles, customization, pets, wasted
game/vendor/three.module.min.js  three.js 0.160 (vendored)
screenshots/       four renders of the game itself
vendor/makefun-game.ts  kit library (buildDossier)
```

## Tech notes

- three.js 0.160, vendored as `game/vendor/three.module.min.js` and
  loaded through an import map (`three` → `./vendor/three.module.min.js`).
- ES modules only, static files only. The game never fetches anything
  over the network — no CDN, no web fonts, no remote assets.
- Seeded randomness (default seed 1): the same seed builds the same
  city, traffic, and pedestrians every run.
- Money is shown with a dollar sign and a thousands separator.
